# Project Brief

AfterFaith Guide helps people questioning, leaving, grieving, or rebuilding after religion find their next safe step.

The site is endpoint-neutral. It supports visitors who are doubting, leaving, staying uncertain, rebuilding secularly, rebuilding spiritually, grieving community loss, navigating family tension, or recovering from religious trauma.

## Mission

Offer calm, practical guidance without mockery, proselytizing, forced atheism, forced reconstruction, debate pressure, or exploitation of trauma.

## Non-Goals

Do not build:

- Monetization
- Ads
- Payments
- Login
- User accounts
- Public comments
- Forums
- Public story submissions
- Admin dashboards
- Newsletter integrations
- Unnecessary tracking

## MVP Surfaces

- Home
- Start Here
- Resources
- Guides
- Scripts
- Support Now
- About / Values
- Suggest a Resource
- Privacy

## Content Model

Guides and scripts are Markdown-backed content collections. Public resources and suggested resource submissions are D1-backed.
