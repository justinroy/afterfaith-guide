# Curation Policy

The resource directory should prioritize safety, clarity, and usefulness.

## Include

- Resource providers with clear public information
- Support organizations with transparent scope
- Books, podcasts, directories, and communities that help people make informed choices
- Secular and religious-trauma-informed resources when they do not pressure a single endpoint

## Review Criteria

- Does it respect user autonomy?
- Does it avoid coercion, harassment, or manipulation?
- Is the cost clear?
- Is the stance clear?
- Is the emotional intensity appropriate to label?
- Are limitations or safety notes needed?

## Exclude

- Resources that exploit trauma
- Resources primarily built around harassment or mockery
- High-pressure conversion or deconversion funnels
- Unclear paid programs
- Communities that encourage unsafe disclosure or public pile-ons
