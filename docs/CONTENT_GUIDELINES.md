# Content Guidelines

## Voice

Write with warmth, clarity, and restraint. Assume readers may be scared, grieving, angry, numb, relieved, or unsure.

## Endpoint Neutrality

Do not frame any single endpoint as the correct outcome. Avoid language that pressures readers toward atheism, religion, spirituality, certainty, family cutoff, reconciliation, therapy, or public disclosure.

## Avoid

- Mockery of religious people
- Debate bait
- Proselytizing
- Trauma sensationalism
- Certainty where nuance is needed
- Prescriptive advice for legal, medical, mental health, or crisis situations

## Prefer

- Options
- Grounding steps
- Clear boundaries
- Safety planning
- Permission to move slowly
- Encouragement to seek qualified support when needed
