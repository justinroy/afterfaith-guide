## Content lives in the repository

Articles, guides, and scripts are Markdown files. Team members can suggest edits through the repository workflow, where changes can be reviewed before publication.

This keeps the public site simple and avoids public accounts, comments, forums, dashboards, newsletters, and extra data collection.

## Review before publishing

Before a page goes live, check that the tone is calm, endpoint-neutral, and practical. Avoid mockery, debate framing, pressure toward one identity, and advice that belongs to a qualified professional.

## Keep the reader’s safety first

When a page discusses family conflict, disclosure, therapy, crisis, abuse, or high-control groups, include privacy and pacing cautions where they help the reader make a safer next choice.
