## Start with the page that matches today

You do not need to read everything before taking care of the next moment. Choose one page based on what is most present today: fear, family pressure, grief, support, therapy, or rebuilding.

If you feel flooded, begin with support and grounding before reading intense stories, debates, or long explanations.

## Use filters as a safety tool

The resource directory includes labels for stage, topic, tradition, type, cost, and intensity. Those labels are not judgments. They are there to help you avoid material that is too much, too soon.

## Leave room to change your mind

Some people rebuild secularly, some spiritually, some religiously, and some stay uncertain for a long time. This library is designed to help you move with care without deciding your whole future today.
