The first months can feel like everything is happening at once. You may be rereading memories, questioning authority, grieving community, and trying to act normal.

Pick a pace that protects your body and your responsibilities. You can limit podcasts, books, online debates, or late-night research if they leave you flooded.

Build a small stability plan: regular sleep, food you can manage, one private place to think, and one support option that does not pressure you.

Avoid making every relationship decision during the highest intensity weeks. Some conversations matter, but timing and preparation matter too.
