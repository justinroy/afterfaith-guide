Holidays can carry memory, beauty, obligation, fear, and loss all at once. You do not have to either reject everything or pretend nothing changed.

Choose what you can participate in honestly and safely. You might keep food, music, candles, service, rest, or family time while declining prayers, services, or conversations that feel coercive.

Make one new ritual small enough to repeat: a walk, a meal, a letter, a donation, a quiet morning, or time with chosen people.
